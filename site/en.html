Frequently asked questions

How does the seven-day trial work?

Activate trial online in Settings → License. Seven days start at the first activation on that PC. Test sales, purchases and available modules; core work can then be local. After expiry new business operations are unavailable until licensed; data, history, viewing, exports and backups remain accessible.

Do I need a permanent internet connection?

No, core accounting and sales are local. Optional services may need internet.

Can OutPOSt control MINI-T400ME?

Full Retail connects via USB/COM, checks device status, reads item database, updates prices, adds missing cards, prints fiscal receipts/returns and produces X/Z reports. Ukrainian hardware/fiscal scenario.

Where is my shop data stored?

Database, settings and backups are stored on your PC separately from program files. Normal update or uninstall does not erase the database.

How many computers does one license cover?

One working PC. Additional PCs need separate licenses; individually arranged discounted terms for six or more.

How do I buy OutPOSt?

Full perpetual license is UAH 2,000 per PC and includes two years of technical support. No checkout or payment on the website. Install trial, contact the author by official email or Telegram, provide Computer ID if requested; payment and issuance are arranged individually.

Is there a free license for Ukrainian veterans?

A verified valid UBD certificate qualifies for one full perpetual license on one PC, same functions and two years of support. Private initiative, not government benefit.

What happens after the two included years?

Program remains active and perpetual license remains valid. The period concerns included technical support.

Can I move my shop database to another PC?

Use controlled backup and restore. Activation is tied to a working PC; replacement of the licensed computer is arranged separately.

Do I need Microsoft Office to use Excel files?

No; XLSX import/export does not require Office. Excel helps move or bulk-edit data and does not replace the local database.

What happens to stock after a sale or purchase?

Posted sales decrease actual stock; posted purchases increase it. Returns, write-offs and stocktake corrections have their own movement history.

Can I use a regular shop laptop?

Designed for Windows 10/11 without separate server infrastructure. Keyboard and barcode workflows are optimized for ordinary work PCs, including lower-powered machines; test your own device.

How do the fiscal integrations differ?

MINI-T400ME is physical USB/COM hardware. Checkbox is a separate PRRO service. Native PRRO is in the full Windows edition, online with file-based electronic signature, guided setup, receipt history and recovery. Current supported scenario is UAH/non-excise goods and services in Ukraine.

Can I try the demo first?

Yes. Seven-day trial requires internet for first activation; reinstalling does not reset it. At expiry, new business operations stop until licensed. Login, viewing, history, reprinting, export, backups and closing an already open local shift remain available. Existing perpetual licenses are unchanged.

Does the full license include future functions?

Full edition is not divided into Standard and Pro. Current and future functions on the licensed PC are included; third-party services/equipment may cost extra.

How are data protected before risky operations?

Maintenance, repair and important migrations use checks and a backup before writing; integrity and critical invariants checked afterward, reject or roll back changes when violated.
