Check discrepancies before adjusting stock

Imagine the recorded stock is 20 packs but the physical count is 18. Confirm the warehouse, barcode, unit and outstanding documents. Enter 18 as the actual count, review the difference of minus 2 and post only after checking it.

Uncounted products are not automatically treated as zero. If a filter hides counted items, posting still includes all counted items. A posted stocktake can be reversed without deleting later movements or history, returned to draft, corrected and posted again.

Catalog and stock movement history

Product cards contain internal codes, units, categories, multiple barcodes, cost and retail/minimum prices, stock thresholds and active states. Shared barcodes prompt a product choice. Posted purchases increase stock; sales decrease it; returns, write-offs, transfers and stocktakes have movement history. Services do not create stock movements. Negative stock remains visible when permitted.

Bulk edits up to 10,000 products use a preview and reject stale data. XLSX import and export work without installed Office; ambiguous rows must be resolved rather than silently imported.

Purchasing workflow (Ukrainian)

Multiple local warehouses

Warehouses are in one local database, not automatically synchronized independent stores or PCs. Draft transfers do not reserve or move stock. Dispatch deducts stock from the sender; only actually received quantities enter the destination, and the rest remains in transit. Only draft cancellation is supported in this workflow; posted dispatch is not reversed here.

Batches, expiry and FEFO

Enable automatic lot tracking explicitly. Do not invent expiry dates. First-expired, first-out (FEFO) control is enabled separately for a product and warehouse after physical reconciliation. Eligible lots with the earliest known expiry are used; expired lots are excluded, and unknown expiry requires explicit permission. If eligible stock is insufficient, a controlled sale is blocked rather than partially posted. Returns restore original lots.

Explicit lot-specific transfer and write-off retain provenance and cost. Lot-specific stocktake and physical-package identification are not claimed as completed workflows.
