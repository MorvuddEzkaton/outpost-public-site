OutPOSt Retail stores core shop data in a local SQLite database on your Windows PC, separate from program files. Everyday local checkout, products, purchasing, stock, debts, reports and history do not need a continuously open browser or a permanent cloud connection.

What works locally

Barcode lookup, cash/card/mixed payment records, sales and returns, customer debt, supplier purchases, stocktake, lot tracking and explicitly enabled FEFO, local director workspace and managerial payroll. Recording a card payment is not processing a bank card. Multiple warehouses in one database are not independent-site synchronization.

Free Lite is a separate edition with one local user and stock location, without fiscalization, printing or online modules. Explore free Lite · Full Retail inventory workflow.

When you need internet

First trial activation, account sign-in and available online services, update checks and downloads, external fiscal services, remote cabinet and online purchasing or Accounting Connector where supported may require connectivity.

Native PRRO is an online Ukrainian workflow for supported UAH non-excise operations. Local checkout does not imply offline fiscalization or regulatory compliance in another country. CheckBox has separate service requirements. eExcise is read-only Sandbox only. Reconcile uncertain fiscal outcomes before retrying.

Back up your own data

Automatic local backups, verified manual backup before risky repair or transfer, another drive or network folder and optional Google Drive copying. Controlled restore and migration are supported; do not assume two independent shop histories can be automatically merged.

Integrity and foreign-key checks and isolated self-test are available. Send a diagnostic package without shop products, customers, PINs or secrets rather than a live database as a first support step. Local storage does not by itself guarantee recovery after disk failure; keep an independent verified backup.

During a power outage

If optional mobile reserve checkout was prepared before the outage, the phone can access previously synchronized product codes, names, retail prices and barcodes. Check the last refresh time. A basket and reserve record can be stored locally until connectivity returns. Without a previously loaded catalog the workflow cannot be guaranteed.

A reserve record is not a fiscal receipt. It does not replace the Windows checkout or RRO/PRRO. Reconcile transaction and fiscal status before posting to avoid duplicate sales or fiscalization.

Try it on your Windows PC

Windows 10/11. Activate the seven-day trial online under Settings → License; reinstalling does not restart the trial. After activation core local workflows can operate offline. After expiry, existing data, history, viewing, reprints, export, backups and closing an open local shift remain available, but new business operations require licensing.
