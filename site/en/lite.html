GETTING STARTED

Set up your shop in OutPOSt Lite

Moving from a notebook or Excel? Start with a small selection of products before transferring your entire catalog.

  1. Prepare the catalog. Add names, barcodes, prices and units. Review your Excel data and remove duplicates before import.
  2. Check stock. Compare the recorded quantities with physical stock. Use purchase sessions for incoming goods and confirm quantities before posting.
  3. Test daily operations. Use separate sample data to try a sale, a return, payment records and stock changes. Create a backup before starting real operations.

Important: Lite does not issue fiscal receipts, connect fiscal devices or process integrated card-terminal payments. Recording card payments is not payment processing. Check local receipt, accounting and tax requirements and arrange any required fiscalization separately.

Frequently asked questions about Lite

Who can use OutPOSt Lite for free?

Anyone. Lite is free without a time limit, registration, license key or proof of status. Download the Windows installer to start local inventory and sales management.

Does the free period expire?

No. Lite is not a trial or the seven-day demo of the full edition. Its own set of features remains available without a time limit.

What features are included?

Sales and returns, cash and card records, cash deposits and withdrawals, purchasing sessions, products and services, sales statistics, local backups and Excel catalog import and export. One local user and one inventory location.

Does Lite include printing, fiscalization or the director portal?

No. Receipt and label printing, fiscalization, the director portal and online modules are not available. The full edition offers additional capabilities for Ukrainian retail. Check the requirements in your own country.

Can I set a PIN?

Yes. You can set an optional 4–12 digit PIN during first launch, or enable, change or disable it later in settings.

Where is Lite data stored?

On your Windows PC, in a separate local Lite database. Full OutPOSt data is stored separately. Create local backups in settings to protect your data.

How do I update Lite?

Use the current official installer from the download section. The full OutPOSt automatic update center does not apply to Lite.

What the veteran benefit includes

The same full application as the standard license: sales, inventory, purchases, customer debts, reports, backups, user permissions, separate Accounting application without an additional module license and supported Ukrainian fiscal integrations. The benefit covers one PC. Additional devices use standard terms; a transfer after hardware replacement is considered individually.

How to request a license

  1. Install the official full edition on the intended PC.
  2. Prepare the Computer ID if requested in Settings → License.
  3. Contact official support to verify eligibility. Do not send sensitive documents to unverified channels.
  4. After issuance, sign into the app using an email code or password. Manual license file import is a compatibility option.

Existing shops and people preparing to start a business may apply if eligible. This private initiative is not a government grant, compensation or cash payment.
