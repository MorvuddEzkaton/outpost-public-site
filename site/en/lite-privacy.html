OutPOSt Lite Privacy Policy

Effective November 3, 2026.

OutPOSt Lite is a local inventory and sales application for Windows. The application is developed by Roman Sapozhnikov, OutPOSt.

Shop data

Products, services, suppliers, purchases, sales, returns and other records that you create or import are stored on your computer. The application works without an online account and without automatically transferring these records to the developer.

PIN, settings and logs

You may optionally set a PIN for access. The application stores a protected derived value of the PIN. This mechanism protects access to the application; the database is stored separately. Settings and diagnostic logs are also stored locally.

Backups and data deletion

You choose where to store backup and exported files and with whom to share them. After uninstalling the application, records remain in a separate data folder. You can retain it for reinstallation or delete it yourself.

Support requests and external services

If you contact support by email, the developer uses the information and attachments you send to handle your request. When you visit external websites or obtain and update the application through the Store, the privacy policies of the respective services apply.

Contact

Questions about privacy and the data you have provided to support can be sent to roman@outpost.com.ua.
