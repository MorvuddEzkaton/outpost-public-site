Checkout and returns

Barcode, internal code and fuzzy name search; deliberately shared barcodes prompt a choice. Multiple open receipts, Ctrl+Tab and Ctrl+1–9, weighted items, fast products, editable quantities and configurable columns.

Record cash with change, card, mixed payment and customer debt. These records do not themselves process a bank card. Discounts respect permissions and minimum prices. Negative stock is permitted only under controlled rules and audited.

Full and partial returns link to the original sale, limit return quantity and restore stock; services create no stock movement. Fiscal returns have their own status and recovery.

Products, services and prices

Product cards contain codes, units, groups, multiple barcodes, cost, retail and minimum prices, stock thresholds, active state and movement history. Services are separate and excluded from stock operations.

Bulk editing of up to 10,000 products with preview; group, unit, activity, tax group, name search/replace, fixed price, percentage and markup changes. Reject stale previews rather than applying changes to a different catalog state.

Controlled XLSX import/export without installed Office. Ambiguous rows are blocked; historical Client-Shop data has a separate controlled import path. UAH price rounding options include 0.01, 0.05, 0.10, 0.25, 0.50 and 1.00.

Purchasing and supplier orders

Purchasing sessions support 12 purchase currencies, including UAH, USD, EUR and TMT. Original price and session exchange rate are retained; cost, supplier debt, stock valuation and retail prices remain in UAH. Later exchange-rate changes do not rewrite posted history.

Replenishment uses demand history, lead time, safety stock, min/max, reorder point, pack size, warehouse and budget. Recommendations and manually selected quantities remain distinct. Drafts do not change stock or debt.

Orders can be edited before dispatch and received partially with actual prices and variance reasons. Remaining quantities stay open. Supplier payments, partial settlements, advances and debt history are controlled separately.

Inventory, batches and debts

Stock movements are recorded separately for sales, purchases, returns, write-offs, stocktakes and transfers. Low, zero and negative stock remain visible. Uncounted products are not silently adjusted; cancelling a posted stocktake creates reverse movements.

Multiple local warehouses, dispatch and partial receipt, goods in transit, lot and expiry tracking, automatic lot creation from purchases, FEFO sales and returns to original lots. Lot-specific transfer and write-off retain history.

Customer debt tracks original sales, partial payments, outstanding balances and returns. Analytics includes overview, products, categories, ABC and stock; comparable periods, saved filters, drill-down and CSV. Net revenue and gross profit account for posted returns.

Three Ukrainian fiscal paths

MINI-T400ME: direct USB/COM control, product and price synchronization, fiscal sales, returns, X/Z reports and recovery. CheckBox: separate optional fiscal integration with queue and recovery.

Native online Ukrainian tax-service PRRO: six-step setup, file-based qualified electronic signature, sales, returns, cash deposits/withdrawals, X/Z reports, history and recovery. Current supported scenario is online UAH transactions for non-excise products and services.

Offline fiscalization and direct bank-terminal control are not claimed for native PRRO. Reconcile uncertain outcomes before retrying. These integrations do not establish tax compliance outside Ukraine.

Permissions, director workspace and payroll

Cashier and administrator permissions cover discounts, purchasing reversal, Excel import/export, data transfer and withdrawals. Menu visibility does not replace authorization checks.

The local director workspace for the active chief administrator includes ten areas: overview, trends, products, categories, cashiers, cash, stock, debts, payroll and local network status. It is distinct from the optional read-only web cabinet.

Managerial payroll supports rates, revenue/profit/payment percentages, progressive bands, targets, bonuses, deductions, advances and limits. Self-payment is disabled by default and requires explicit permissions. This is not regulated payroll or tax calculation.

Local data, backups and updates

The SQLite working database is separate from Program Files. Automatic local and verified manual backups, selected drive/network location and optional Google Drive copying; controlled restore and transfer. Separate shop histories are not automatically merged.

Integrity and foreign-key checks, isolated self-tests, diagnostic support package without products, customers, PINs or secrets. Controlled repair uses a backup, transaction rollback and before/after audit.

Website Retail checks signed update metadata and package integrity, applies compatible updates on safe normal exit, and retains full installer fallback. Signed update metadata is not an Authenticode signature on the standalone installer. Keep a verified backup before updating.

Eight optional workflows

Supplier orders; automated replenishment; multiple local warehouses; promotions and loyalty with rules, promo codes, bonuses, returns and statistics.

Price tags and labels: batch printing, Code128, templates, WPF editor, undo/redo, calibration and JSON import/export. Batches and expiry with FEFO; advanced analytics; optional read-only web cabinet and network view.

License, interface and getting started

Full Retail costs UAH 2,000 once per working PC, perpetual license and two years of technical support. Additional PCs need separate activation; six or more PCs have individually arranged terms. Eligible Ukrainian UBD holders may receive one free full license after verification.

English, Ukrainian and Russian interface and help; light, dark and high-contrast themes, configurable text, buttons, columns and window positions. F1 opens local help.

Seven-day trial starts after first online activation. Sign in via Settings → License with password or email code. Free Lite is a separate edition for one user and one stock location with no registration or time limit, without printing, fiscalization or online modules.
