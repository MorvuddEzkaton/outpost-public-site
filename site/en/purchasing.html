Recommendations and drafts do not automatically become posted purchases.

Ordered 20 packs, received 12. What about the other 8?

In this illustrative example, the shop orders 20 packs. The draft does not add stock or create supplier debt. At the first delivery, confirm only the 12 packs physically received. The other 8 remain outstanding until the next confirmed receipt, unless explicitly rejected.

Verify actual quantity, price, unit, supplier and warehouse before posting. OutPOSt does not automatically record undelivered goods as stock. Internal warehouse transfers use their own documents.

Plan stock replenishment

Use actual sales history, available stock, already ordered quantities, supplier lead time, safety stock, target stock days or min/max, reorder point, fixed order quantity, pack size, warehouse and budget. Keep the recommended quantity distinct from a manually chosen quantity.

Review the preview before creating one draft per supplier. Exceeding the budget blocks saving with an explanation. OutPOSt does not automatically send an order to a supplier; the user confirms the actual ordering status after arranging the purchase.

Receive and reconcile supplier orders

Edit draft quantities, purchase prices, supplier product codes, expected dates and notes. During receiving, explicitly accept, reject or continue waiting for each item. The actual purchase price can differ from the draft; rejected quantities require a reason.

Only accepted quantities create a posted purchase and update inventory and cost. Outstanding quantities remain available for future delivery. Supplier payments, advances, partial settlements and debt history are handled separately; purchasing permissions distinguish viewing, editing and posting.

Inventory and stocktaking guide

Purchase currencies and historical exchange rates

Purchasing supports 12 base currencies, including UAH, USD, EUR and TMT. Store the original currency price and the exchange rate of each document. Later rate changes do not rewrite posted history.

Cost, supplier debt, stock valuation and retail prices remain in UAH. Currency selection is not a claim of multi-currency accounting or foreign fiscal compliance. Remote purchasing requires the relevant online setup and staff confirmation on the PC; it does not automatically post goods.

Purchasing, batches and expiry dates

When explicitly enabled, posted purchases create lots linked to their source rows and actual costs. FEFO control is separately enabled for a product and warehouse after physical reconciliation. Expired lots are excluded from FEFO sales; unknown expiry requires explicit permission. Returns restore original lots.

Lot-specific transfers and write-offs require explicit allocation. Lot-specific stocktaking and physical-package identification are not claimed as completed workflows. See the stocktake workflow.
