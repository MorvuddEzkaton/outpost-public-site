OutPOSt Lite — free edition

Can I use OutPOSt for free without Ukrainian veteran status?

Yes. OutPOSt Lite is free indefinitely for everyone: sales, purchasing, products and services, basic statistics and cash deposits and withdrawals, one local user and one stock location. No registration or activation. Printing, fiscalization and online modules are available in the full edition.

Where can I set a PIN and how do I report a Lite error?

Set a PIN on first launch or in Lite settings. Include OutPOSt Lite, its version and the exact error message in your request. The current installer is in the separate Lite download section.

Licensing and payment

How much does OutPOSt cost?

The full license costs UAH 2,000 once per working PC. It is perpetual and remains active after the included support period ends.

Is OutPOSt free for Ukrainian veterans with UBD status?

A holder of a valid Ukrainian combatant-status certificate (UBD) may receive one free perpetual full license for one working PC after verification. The same functions and two years of technical support are included. Standard terms apply to additional PCs.

How many computers does a license cover?

One license covers one working PC. Each additional PC needs its own activation. Discounted terms for six or more PCs are arranged individually.

Is there a monthly subscription?

There is no monthly subscription for the OutPOSt license itself. Optional third-party services may charge separately.

How long is support included?

Technical support is included for two years from standard or eligible veteran license issuance. The perpetual license remains valid afterward.

How can I move my license to another PC?

Activation is tied to a working PC. Replacement or transfer is considered individually. Contact support and explain why the PC is being replaced.

Ukrainian fiscal integration and CheckBox

What does native PRRO support?

Full OutPOSt includes an online Ukrainian tax-service PRRO: six-step setup, file-based electronic signature, sales and returns, cash deposits and withdrawals, X/Z reports and receipt history. The supported scenario is online UAH transactions for non-excise goods and services. Register the cash register and cashier separately with the tax service. Offline fiscalization is not supported.

Can I use several PRROs or sole proprietorships?

The architecture supports multiple PRROs and business entities. The specific cash-register arrangement and routing rules are configured for the shop.

Do I need an electronic signature?

For native tax-service PRRO, use the appropriate signer's file-based qualified electronic signature and a registered cash register. The six-step wizard allows key configuration and connection checks. The current workflow uses a file key, not a Diia.Signature confirmation for every receipt.

CheckBox or native OutPOSt PRRO?

These are separate fiscal options. Native online PRRO is included in the full Windows application, with setup, history and recovery. CheckBox remains a separate integration. Native PRRO requires tax-service registration and a file-based qualified signature. A bank terminal is separate.

What if fiscalization fails?

Check receipt history or the hardware queue first. If the tax service or device result is uncertain, do not create a new receipt or resend blindly. OutPOSt retains state for reconciliation and recovery. Report the version, exact error and operation status.

MINI-T400ME hardware

Does OutPOSt support MINI-T400ME?

Full OutPOSt controls MINI-T400ME via USB/COM, including products, fiscal sales, returns and X/Z reports. Changing the payment method of a confirmed receipt involves a linked return followed by a new sale after confirmation. Reconcile an unknown device result first.

What if MINI-T400 is not detected?

Check power, cable and COM port. OutPOSt can search available COM ports and typical connection speeds. If no connection is found, report the error and include a screenshot.

Are products and prices synchronized?

The MINI-T400 workflow writes product data and checks the result after synchronization to reduce discrepancies between software and hardware.

Separate Accounting pre-release

Is OutPOSt Accounting ready?

A separate Windows Accounting pre-release is available with successful Windows RC, regression, installer and database recovery checks. The installer is not Authenticode-signed; SmartScreen may warn. Real PC and database acceptance is separate.

Does Accounting cost extra?

OutPOSt Accounting is included in the main package without a separate module license and installs as a separate Windows application.

Can data be transferred from Retail?

Yes. The separate Accounting application supports import and exchange with Retail through controlled batch import and optional background operation while the application is open. Received records require a separate posting action by the accountant; ambiguous events stay under review.

Is Accounting suitable for Ukrainian sole proprietors?

It is intended for accounting records of Ukrainian sole proprietors and retail shops as well. It is not a complete regulated reporting, electronic signature or tax-return filing suite; verify required forms and reporting methods separately.

Director portal and managerial payroll

What is the local director workspace?

A separate local workspace inside OutPOSt for the active chief administrator, with ten areas: overview, trends, products, categories, cashiers, cash, stock, debts, payroll and local network status.

Are the local director workspace and web cabinet the same?

No. The local director workspace is part of the Windows application. The separate web cabinet and network workflow provide read-only aggregate viewing and do not replace it.

How does cashier payroll work?

Managerial payroll can combine period, monthly, daily, hourly or shift rates; percentages of net revenue, gross profit or payments; progressive ranges, targets, bonuses, deductions, advances and limits. Work time is tied to the employee who opened the cash shift. This is not statutory payroll or tax calculation.

Can cashiers pay themselves from the cash register?

Not by default. Only the chief administrator can separately permit self-payment. The cashier then needs their own open shift and cash-withdrawal permission, and can view only their own current-day calculation. A changed amount needs a reason, and payment requires explicit confirmation.

Ukrainian eExcise / XTrace — Sandbox only

What is eExcise / XTrace in OutPOSt?

An optional read-only Sandbox module for controlled reading of economic operators, objects and reference data, plus single-mark informational verification. Production and remote writes are blocked.

Does Data Matrix scanning work?

Single electronic-mark verification is integrated in the mark window. It is informational and read-only; it does not activate or redeem a mark or send an electronic excise document.

Can I connect eExcise now?

Not as a production connection. A local read-only Sandbox workflow is available; full authorized acceptance requires a current Sandbox token. POST, PUT, PATCH, DELETE and production remain blocked.

Updates, database and backups

Where can I download the latest version?

The official download page provides Retail, free Lite and separate Accounting installers. The universal Retail downloader retrieves the current build. The Telegram bot also has a current versions menu.

How do I learn about new versions?

In full OutPOSt, click the version number to open the Update Center. It checks signed metadata and downloads changed files when compatible, retaining a full installer fallback. Automatic updates can be disabled; application takes place during safe normal exit. Official downloads and optional Telegram update notifications are also available.

Should I back up before updating?

Yes. Keep a verified backup for recovery and rollback. Current Retail installation no longer requires an additional full database archive before updating; ordinary backups remain separate. The local database and perpetual license remain on the PC.

Reserve checkout during power cuts

What if a power cut makes the main checkout PC unavailable?

If reserve checkout was prepared in advance, a phone can search the local catalog, show the last synchronized price, build a basket and save a reserve record. Reconcile and process records in the main application when the Windows checkout returns.

Does reserve checkout work without mobile internet?

After prior preparation, the catalog is stored on the phone and may remain available during temporary connectivity loss. Reserve records stay local until synchronization. Availability is not guaranteed without prior preparation. Check the last update time when connectivity returns.

Is a reserve checkout record a fiscal receipt?

No. A phone reserve record is not a fiscal receipt and does not replace RRO or PRRO. Reconcile transaction and fiscal status before processing it in the main checkout to avoid duplicate sales or fiscalization.

Errors and support requests

How do I report an error?

Use the report-error option or contact official support. In one message include the version, what you were doing and the exact error text. Attach a screenshot or diagnostic file if useful, after checking for sensitive information.

What information should I include?

Provide the OutPOSt edition and version, exact error text, actions immediately before it and a screenshot if possible. For MINI-T400ME also include the COM port and connection-check result.

Do I need to send my shop database?

Usually no. Start with a diagnostic package and technical description. Do not send products, customer records, PINs, secrets or a complete SQLite database unless a specific need and secure transfer have been agreed.

What if the fiscal result is uncertain?

Do not retry the receipt or return blindly. Check the queue or recovery state and record the error. First determine whether the external fiscal service or hardware already created the document.
