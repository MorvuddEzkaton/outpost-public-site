Activate your full license in four steps

  1. Download and install the current Retail build from the official download guide.
  2. Activate the seven-day trial online in Settings → License and test your own workflow on the target PC.
  3. Contact the developer to arrange license issuance. Support may request the Computer ID of the working PC and, for UBD, verification of eligibility. Do not email passwords, PINs or electronic-signature private keys.
  4. In Settings → License sign in with your account password or one-time email code, without a browser. An already issued license for this PC and available online services connect automatically. Manual Computer ID and .outpost-license file import remain compatibility options.

Signing out does not delete the local perpetual license or shop data. Replacement of a licensed PC is handled individually. Core local work does not require a permanent online license check.

English support and troubleshooting

License and activation questions

What happens after two years of support?

The perpetual license stays active on the licensed PC. Two years refers to included technical support and assistance, not the duration of your right to use the software.

Can one license activate two PCs?

No. Each working PC requires its own activation. Discounted terms for six or more PCs are agreed individually. Contact support about replacing a licensed PC.

Is there a subscription or a Standard and Pro plan?

No. The full OutPOSt license is a single product without a monthly license fee or Standard and Pro tiers. Optional external services, electronic signatures and equipment can cost extra.

Are future updates included?

The current license policy provides access to product features and updates without moving between Standard and Pro tiers. This does not include third-party service fees or extend the included support period.

How does the seven-day trial work?

Activate trial access online in Settings → License. Seven days start at the first activation on that PC; reinstalling does not reset the period. Core operations can then work offline. After expiry, new business operations are unavailable until licensed; existing data, history, viewing, reprinting, export, backups and closing an already open local shift remain available.

Is the veteran license the same as the full edition?

Yes. A verified holder of a valid Ukrainian combatant-status certificate (UBD) may receive one free perpetual full license for one working PC with the same features and two years of technical support. Additional PCs follow standard terms. This is a private initiative, not a government grant.

Can I use OutPOSt without a paid license?

Yes. OutPOSt Lite is free indefinitely for everyone, without registration, activation or a time limit. It supports one local user and stock location, but does not include printing, fiscalization or online modules.
